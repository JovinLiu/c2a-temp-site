# Joinery Installation Agreement editor

The third editor is based on the supplied Joinery Installation Agreement and is intentionally limited to installation services.

It includes editable bilingual fields for:

- Contractor, Client and installation address
- Scope of works and exclusions
- Commencement and delay provisions
- Contract sum and GST wording
- Two payment stages and the optional retained final balance
- Bank details
- Five-year workmanship warranty and quality statement
- Variations
- Client and Contractor responsibilities
- Signatures

It does not include product supply, joinery transportation, stone supply or installation quotations, or the Schedule 1 quotation forms used by the Supply & Installation Agreement.

The Chinese wording is an editable working translation supplied for document preparation. The final agreement should be reviewed for the relevant project and, where appropriate, by an Australian legal professional before signing.
